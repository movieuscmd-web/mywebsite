PAGE 1-4 - QUANTUM NUMBERS

Quantum Numbers

n: Principal (1,2,3...), size & energy | l: Azimuthal (0 to n-1), shape s=0,p=1,d=2,f=3 | m: Magnetic (-l to +l), orientation | s: Spin (+1/2,-1/2)

n > l ≥ m | l=0→s, 1→p, 2→d, 3→f

Zeeman effect (magnetic field me splitting) & Stark effect (electric field me splitting) - degeneracy khatam hoti hai
PAGE 5-8 - PAULI, HUND, AUFBAU

1. Pauli Exclusion:

Ek orbital me max 2e⁻, opposite spin (↑↓)

2. Hund's Rule:

Degenerate orbitals me pehle ek-ek unpaired (↑ ↑ ↑), fir pairing. Jyada unpaired = jyada stable

3. Aufbau - (n+l) Rule:

Chhota (n+l) pehle bharega, (n+l) same ho to chhota n pehle. Order: 1s <2s<2p<3s<3p<4s<3d<4p<5s<4d<5p<6s<4f<5d<6p<7s<5f<6d<7p

Correct filling: [Xe] <6s² <5d¹4f¹⁻¹⁴ <5d²⁻¹⁰ <6p⁶ <7s² <6d¹5f¹⁻¹⁴ <6d²⁻¹⁰...
PAGE 9-12 - ELECTRONIC CONFIGURATION

Stability Reason: Exchange + Pairing Energy

Pairing Energy (+P): ↑↓ banane ke liye energy lagti hai (+ve)

Exchange Energy (-E): Same spin wale electrons exchange kare to energy release (-ve)

No. of Exchanges = n(n-1)/2 | n=same spin e⁻
↑↑↑↑↑ = 5*4/2=10 → -10E (4+3+2+1)
More negative energy = More stable | Less energy = More stable
PAGE 12-15 - Cr & Cu PROOF - MOST IMPORTANT

Cr (24) Proof:

Incorrect: [Ar] 3d⁴4s² → [↑][↑][↑][↑][ ] [↑↓] → Exchanges 4*3/2=6 → Energy = -6E + P
Correct: [Ar] 3d⁵4s¹ → [↑][↑][↑][↑][↑] [↑] → Exchanges 5*4/2=10 → Energy = -10E + 0P → MORE STABLE
Isliye Cr = 3d⁵4s¹

Cu (29) Proof:

Incorrect: [Ar] 3d⁹4s² → [↑↓][↑↓][↑↓][↑↓][↑] [↑↓] → Up 10 + Down 6 = -16E +5P
Correct: [Ar] 3d¹⁰4s¹ → [↑↓][↑↓][↑↓][↑↓][↑↓] [↑] → Up 10 + Down 10 = -20E +5P → MORE STABLE
Isliye Cu = 3d¹⁰4s¹
PAGE 15-17 - SHAPES OF ORBITALS

s-orbital:

Spherical, Ψ² all direction equal, gerade (g), No nodal plane for 1s, lobes=1

p-orbital: 3-fold degenerate, dumb-bell, axial

OrbitalmNodal PlaneShape
px-1yz planealong x
py0xz planealong y
pz+1xy planealong z

(+) (-) = sign of wave function Ψ, not charge! Ungerade ψ(u) - odd nodal

d-orbital: 5-fold degenerate, double dumb-bell

t2g (non-axial) b/w axeseg (axial) along axes
dxy, dyz, dzx → density b/w axesdx²-y², dz² → density along axes

Gerade ψ(g) = even nodal plane (2), Ungerade ψ(u)=odd (1)

dz²: most different, 0 nodal plane + 2 nodal cone, axial eg

f-orbital: 7-fold degenerate, m=-3 to +3, shape not in syllabus

NODE FORMULAS - JEE HAR SAAL:

Angular node / Nodal plane = l (except dz²) | Lobes = 2l (except dz²)
Radial node = n-l-1 | Total nodes = Radial+Angular = n-1

Ex: 3d (n=3,l=2) → Angular=2, Radial=0, Total=2

Degenerate: free atom me same energy (px=py=pz, d ke 5 same) | Non-degenerate: field lagne par energy alag (eg upar, t2g niche) → Yahi Crystal Field Splitting hai!

PAGE 20 - MAGNETIC MOMENT - FINAL PAGE

Paramagnetic (μ ≠ 0): atleast 1 unpaired, attracted by magnet

Diamagnetic (μ = 0): no unpaired, repelled

μ = √n(n+2) B.M. (Bohr Magneton)
n = no. of unpaired e⁻
nμ
